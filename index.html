Frequently Asked Questions

What are houseboat rates in Kumarakom?

Houseboat rates vary depending on the category (1 BHK to 4 BHK), season, and whether you choose a day cruise or an overnight stay. Contact us on WhatsApp or submit a request to get the best customized rates for your travel dates.

How do I make a house boat booking in Kumarakom?

Fill in the quote form or message us on WhatsApp. You’ll get availability and a quote in 15–30 minutes.

What are the timings for an overnight stay vs a day trip cruise?

Check-in for overnight cruises is at 12:00 PM noon with check-out at 09:30 AM the next morning. A day trip cruise runs from 11:00 AM to 04:00 PM.

How many people can stay on each boat?

Maximum occupancy per boat category:

  • 1 BHK: Up to 4 guests (including children)
  • 2 BHK: Up to 8 guests (including children)
  • 3 BHK: Up to 12 guests (including children)
  • 4 BHK: 16+ guests (including children)

Are there any extra charges?

Fuel, captain, chef, and 4 meals are included in your package. Additional charges may only apply for extra guests beyond capacity or specific extra requests.

Where is the boarding point for the house boat?

Boarding is at Cheeppunkal Jetty (Near St. Antony's Church, under Cheeppunkal Bridge, Kumarakom 686563). Free secure parking is available for guest cars.
