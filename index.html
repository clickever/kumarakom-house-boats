Quick FAQs

What are the cruise timings?

Check-in is at 12:00 PM noon and check-out is at 09:30 AM the next morning. Day cruises run from 11:00 AM to 04:00 PM.

What is included in the price?

Your full rate includes the entire private boat, fuel, captain, chef, and 4 meals (lunch, tea/snacks, dinner, breakfast). There are no hidden fees.

Where is the boarding point?

Boarding is at Cheeppunkal Jetty (Near St. Antony's Church, under Cheeppunkal Bridge, Kumarakom 686563). Free secure parking is available for guest cars.
